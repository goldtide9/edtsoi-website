Founders that have inspired me

Just to name a few...

Bipul Sinha (Rubrik): Deeply inspired by his Maximal Thinking framework. Startup is full of contradictions. Maximal thinking allows me to think big enough to solve really hard problems, while maximizing present opportunities to minimize stress.

Jeff Bezos: Learned and agreed with him on the 70% rule, which advocates for making decisions when you have 70% of the information you need, rather than waiting for 100% certainty, which could lead to missed opportunities and slow progress.
