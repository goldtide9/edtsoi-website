Founder Paradox

A question that I get asked a lot is, if 99% of startups fail, why do people still start and fully dedicate themselves to them?

The short answer is rather "obvious" in my opinion -- founders deeply passionate about innovating and solving problems, and believing in that one day they will become that 1% (or 0.1%).

 

Easier said than done right? But how do founders become that top 1% or 0.1%?

I believe the secret is to fully embrace the founder paradox.
